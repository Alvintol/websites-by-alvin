A FEW FAIR QUESTIONS

Before you reach out.

What kind of website do you build?+

I’m starting with focused websites for local and service-based small businesses: a clear homepage, services, business information, and an easy way for customers to get in touch.

How much will it cost?+

Every business needs something slightly different. I keep the first scope small and practical, then provide a clear quote before work begins. Extra features are discussed separately.

Do I need to know anything technical?+

No. I’ll explain domains, hosting, and updates in plain language and handle the technical setup we agree on.

What do I need to provide?+

Your business details, services, service area, contact information, and any logo or photos you already have. I’ll help organize the information into a useful page.

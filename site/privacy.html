1 Who we are

This website is operated by Created by Alvin, a registered trade name of Alvin Tolentino (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy explains what personal information this website collects, and what it does not.

2 What this website does not do

As currently built, this website does not have a contact form, account system, or newsletter signup, and does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. It does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to us through your browser.

3 How to contact us

The email links on this site open your own email application; some start a pre-filled draft for you to edit. Any information you choose to include — such as your name, business name, and enquiry details — is sent directly to inbox@createdbyalvin.com through your own email provider. This website itself does not receive, collect, or store that message; it exists only in your email account and ours.

We use information you send us only to respond to your enquiry and, if you choose to proceed, to prepare a quote and project agreement. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

4 Live template previews

This site displays live previews of example website templates hosted on separate createdbyalvin.com subdomains (for example, neonmodular.createdbyalvin.com). Viewing a preview loads that subdomain's content in your browser. Those demonstration sites are built to show design work and do not themselves collect personal information as currently built, but this policy covers only the main createdbyalvin.com site — if that changes, the relevant subdomain will carry its own notice.

5 Applicable law

We handle personal information in accordance with Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and other Canadian privacy laws that apply to our business.

6 Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you (for example, in past enquiry emails) or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing inbox@createdbyalvin.com.

7 Changes to this policy

If we add a contact form, analytics, cookies, or any other feature that collects personal information through this website, we will update this policy to describe it before that feature goes live.
